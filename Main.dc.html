About Nitin

Nitin Srivastava is a techno-commercial sales, marketing and business development professional with more than 23 years in automotive components, including over 15 years in leadership roles at leading Indian and multinational companies. He is currently Head Sales & Marketing (Group Marketing Head) at Munjal Auto Industries Limited.

Outside work he writes as writernitin, the pen name of a writer, singer, poet and digital content creator who works in both Hindi and English. Public speaking and storytelling are part of that practice.

His stories stay close to feeling: the breakup that does not end, the relationship that changes shape, the night that stays with you. He writes them short enough to finish in one sitting and honest enough to remember afterwards.

His books and stories are available on Amazon and Pratilipi, and he shares new writing, poetry and music on social media under the handle @writernitin.
